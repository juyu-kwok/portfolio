Teaching Practice & Classroom Case Studies

Case Study 01

Kids Mandarin Summer Camp

Mandarin Teacher

Chinese Culture University · Division of Continuing Education · Taipei, Taiwan

Programme
Three-week intensive Mandarin immersion
Learners
Multinational beginner students, ages 6–9
Class Size
10–12 students
Teaching Period
Summers 2023, 2024 & 2026

Designed and delivered three-week intensive Mandarin immersion programmes for multinational beginner learners aged 6–9. The curriculum combined stories, songs, games, TPR and structured speaking activities with two culminating projects: a collaborative Book Creator eBook built from a teacher-designed template, through which students contributed original drawings and voice recordings; and an original Mandarin musical showcase integrating storytelling, music and movement.

Learning was progressively scaffolded from supported listening and speaking to personal expression, oral retelling and performance. Daily ClassDojo updates and portfolio sharing provided families with ongoing visibility into student learning.

Learning through Stories, Songs & Games

Songs, TPR, stories and classroom games provided repeated exposure to new vocabulary and sentence patterns in an immersive learning environment.

Structured Opportunities to Speak

Role-play, Turn-and-Talk and Show-and-Tell gave students regular opportunities to use Mandarin for meaningful classroom communication.

Story Comprehension & Oral Retelling

Students moved from shared story listening to individual drawing, teacher conferencing and oral retelling, with support adjusted to individual language proficiency.

Collaborative eBook Project

Using a teacher-designed Book Creator template, students contributed voice recordings and original drawings to a shared class eBook. The project gave beginner learners a clear structure for speaking while also allowing individual creativity through their artwork and the experience of contributing to a collective class product.

Student Work

Students completed a structured “All About Me” speaking task using language learned during the first two weeks of the programme. Using the same Book Creator template, oral output was differentiated according to age and language proficiency, while students added their own drawings to personalise their contribution.

Scaffolded Performance Project

I scripted each performance around the language level of the class, often using a Mandarin song as the starting point for the story. Students first built understanding through story work, vocabulary and speaking activities, then practised dialogue and songs, retold key parts of the story, and finally performed on stage. The sequence was designed so that performance grew from understanding rather than script memorisation.

Performance Outcomes across Three Summer Programmes

Final performances formed a recurring learning outcome across the three summer programmes, giving beginner learners a concrete purpose for practising spoken Mandarin and an opportunity to present their learning to an audience.

Case Study 02

Early Years Mandarin at Bina Bangsa School

Preschool Mandarin Teacher · MOE Overseas Teacher Programme

Bina Bangsa School · Kebon Jeruk Campus · Jakarta, Indonesia

School Context
Trilingual international school
Learners
Pre-Nursery to Kindergarten 2 · Ages 2–6
Programme
Thematic Mandarin immersion within the early years curriculum
Teaching Period
July 2024 – June 2025

Delivered thematic Mandarin learning experiences for children from Pre-Nursery to Kindergarten 2 within a trilingual international school. Lessons combined stories, songs, movement, TPR, games, visual materials and hands-on activities to support participation across different developmental stages.

My role extended beyond regular classroom teaching. I planned and led a school-wide Mid-Autumn Festival Open Day, led a cross-border online cultural exchange with a preschool in Taiwan, and contributed to a cross-disciplinary Student-Led Conference involving families. The experience strengthened my understanding of early childhood communication, classroom routines, family engagement and collaborative planning.

Learning through Play

Stories, songs, movement, TPR, role-play and hands-on activities helped young children connect new Mandarin with actions, images and familiar classroom experiences.

Mid-Autumn Festival Open Day

Planned and led the school-wide Mid-Autumn Festival Open Day for 200+ parents, coordinating interactive station games, logistics and student cultural performances with other teachers.

Chinese New Year Culture Week & Open Day

Worked with the Mandarin teaching team on the school’s Chinese New Year Open Day. I coordinated the preschool performance programme, helped prepare the main stage, and provided teaching resources for the preschool’s week-long Chinese culture theme. Families joined the celebration through New Year greetings, dumpling making and other cultural activities.

Cross-Border Cultural Exchange

Led a cross-border online cultural exchange programme with a preschool in Taiwan, coordinating directly with the partner teacher and managing the programme from planning through implementation.

Student-Led Conference: Zoo Station

As part of a cross-disciplinary Student-Led Conference designed as a simulated trip to China, I created the zoo station, where children helped family members locate missing animals using Mandarin. I also designed a simple Tic-Tac-Toe extension activity for families to use while waiting between stations.

Creative Writing Awards & Publications

Creative Writing Awards & Publications

I have always been drawn to children’s literature and animation, particularly stories that preserve a sense of curiosity, imagination and a child’s point of view. Writing has remained a personal interest alongside my professional work, and several of my stories have received literary awards or been published. Storytelling also finds its way naturally into my teaching, where I use narrative to give language a meaningful context for listening, speaking, creating and performing.
